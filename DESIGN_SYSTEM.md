# Shared classroom visual system

## Purpose

One legible, restrained presentation system for every subject. The style is owned by the renderer; lesson plans provide the meaning. It is not a separate hand-designed KML template per topic.

## Visual hierarchy

1. Topic/stage title: prominent bold type.
2. Main question or explanation: readable regular type; text shrinks only within bounded limits and overflows produce an error.
3. One next action: mint accent card with an explicit reply destination.
4. Map legend and factual sources: secondary but consistently placed.
5. Time/context and schematic disclosures: subdued supporting text.

A navy background, off-white text, mint interface accent and six fixed feature colours are shared across the panel and map. Rounded corners, fine separators and quiet spacing replace heavy nested borders. Explanation diagrams occupy a separate column and stay out of question stages.

## Map rules

- Thin double-stroke rings provide contrast over imagery without thick outlines.
- Arrowheads are capped using camera range and segment length; long authored shafts do not create giant heads.
- Only point locations get map labels, with a maximum of four. Movement and area descriptions are in the panel legend.
- Schematic lines stay visually lighter than sourced lines. A lighter style does not make guessed geography accurate.
- North-up is default. Explicit intentional rotation and the existing orbit are supported.
- Geometry positions and extent remain plan-authored; the renderer never invents a plate boundary, measured area or archaeological feature.

## Compatibility and limits

Shared styling updates active saved plans through continue. It does not require clearing records or rebuilding a lesson. Pipeline and end cleanup are unchanged. The font assets include their license. Native Earth navigation controls and cropped VM viewports are not controlled by this visual system. New live marker/arrow visibility, perspective and readability require a rig check.

## Local validation

Every panel phase and explanation beat was rendered using the reviewed river plan, prepared climate plan and the contributor-generated earthquake plan. The comparison preview uses their actual content, not mock observations. Native Earth rendering is not available in the development environment.
