# Submission preparation

## What the skill contributes

The Globe Is Your Classroom turns an LG explanation into a conversation: predict, investigate, explain and apply. Nara interprets causal reasoning, the helper persists attempts and selects controlled visual stages, and a new location tests application under a changed condition. It extends existing educational visualization with explicit learner-response branching and resumable progression. It does not claim that existing skills cannot do teaching.
